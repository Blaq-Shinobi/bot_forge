FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any coding experience?

Not at all! BotForge is designed for non-technical users. Everything is configured through a visual interface with simple forms and drag-and-drop.

How fast can I set up a bot?

Most users have their first bot running in under 5 minutes. Just connect your BotFather token, configure a few responses, and deploy.

Can I use my own server?

Pro and Business plans include the option to export your bot configuration as Python code that you can self-host if you prefer.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

Yes! Both Pro and Business plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.

Can bots accept payments?

Yes. Business plan bots can integrate with Telegram Payments, Stripe, and other processors to accept payments directly in chat.
