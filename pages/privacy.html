Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a starting template, not legal advice - it hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer. Given BotForge handles accounts, bot tokens, and payments, it's worth having one look this over before you rely on it, especially for anything specific to your country's data protection law.

What we collect

When you create an account: your name, email address, and a hashed (never plain-text) version of your password.

When you build a bot: the bot's Telegram token, its name and description, and whatever commands, auto-replies, welcome messages, and scheduled messages you configure for it.

When your bot runs: the messages Telegram delivers to it are processed to generate responses. We don't read or store this traffic beyond what's needed to run your bot's configured behavior.

When you subscribe to a paid plan: payment is handled by Paystack. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded and a reference/subscription code - we do not receive or store your card details.

How we use it

  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To run the bots you've configured, exactly as you configured them.
  • To enforce plan limits (bot count, messages per day, and similar).
  • To communicate with you about your account or changes to the service.

What we share

We share what's necessary to run the service and nothing more: your bot's messages go to Telegram (since that's how your bot talks to its users), and payment details go to Paystack (since that's who processes them). We don't sell personal data to third parties.

Data retention

We keep your account and bot configuration for as long as your account is active. If you delete a bot or your account, its configuration is removed from our systems, subject to any backups that age out on their own schedule.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account entirely. Contact us (see the Contact page) to make any of these requests.

Security

Passwords are hashed, not stored in plain text. Bot tokens are sensitive credentials - we're actively working on additional protections for how they're stored (encryption at rest) given they grant control over your Telegram bot.

Children's privacy

BotForge isn't intended for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and let you know.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via the Contact page.
